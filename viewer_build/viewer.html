TOOLBAR
📍GPS: off / pin / follow / heading-up · record a track · drop pins · export GPX/KML · compass calibration
🔧Tools: find (stations, leads — active / closed), measure, route, reach, fly, crane, import KML · 📐 field survey · 📚 documents · 📝 change log (undo)
🚩Route: tap the start station, then the end station (only stations answer: not a lead, a name or a pin), or type part of a name in the box under the bar at the top, with the cave in the list. With a route showing only its stations can be tapped; the rest of the cave, its names and leads are dimmed until you clear it (✕ on the bar).
🎨Display: colour by survey / elevation / depth / difficulty (narrow blue, low red, steep green); walls (On: one see-through layer, the nearest, at the opacity you set · Ghost: see-through, every layer, with sliders for strength, edges and depth fade · Off); stations (with their names); show or hide leads, inventories, area names, tracks, imported layers; imagery and terrain; Focus
🎯Focus (the small button under ⋯, or Display → Focus): Near centre keeps walls, lines, stations and names within a radius of the orbit point (the middle of the screen) as they are and fades the rest; Cut in front cuts away the passages between you and the orbit point, a hole as wide as the cut radius there. Taps only find what is in focus. Turn the button off to see everything again. The wall sliders and focus settings are remembered for this map too.

STATIONS
👆Tap: info · again: caver for scale · again: reach (what's within range along the survey) · again: off
✏️On a station's card: ✏️ Add note is always there · 📄 its documents (see Documents below; the ones in the export too: scanned notes and photos; PDFs stay in FLEX). Everything else — changing a note, ❓ leads (status, size…), 📐 survey new passage from here, and in a document turning pages, tags, removing — waits behind the small ✎ Edit button, which is off again when you close the card.
👆Taps find the nearest station, lead mark, name or pin within a finger's width of the touch; a drag or a two-finger move is never a tap
DOCUMENTS
📄A station's card lists its documents in four groups, each with a count: Survey notes, Sketches, Media, Other. Most specific first: the sketch page that covers the station (a page tagged A6~A8) or the book that holds it; if there are none, its survey's; then the cave's; never another survey's or cave's. A group that comes from a wider level says so (“Survey notes (1) · from survey T”). A document opens at the page that was found (“page 2: stations A2–A4”). The project's untagged documents wait behind “Show N not yet sorted…”.
〰️Tap the survey or the cave on a station's card (or 📚 → Surveys) for its own card: the survey's documents, or the cave's when it has none. 📚 lists every document by group, with counts.
🏷Tagging waits behind ✎ Edit in a document: 🏷 Tags for the whole document, 🏷 Tag this page… for one page (a station, or a range such as A6~A8: A6 to A8 and A6–A8 mean the same). Pick the cave and the survey, type the station or the range (the map's stations are suggested, Enter takes the first); recent tags are one tap away; Done saves them together, undo is in 📝 Change log. The caption says what the page is tagged to (“This page: stations A6–A8”).
📷A station whose shots were read from a scanned page says From page 2 of “…”: tap it to see that page. Pages and photos you add get a kind from their name (a camera photo is Media, a scan Survey notes, a PDF Other); change it in ✎ Edit → ⋯ → Title, kind, date.
FIELD SURVEY
📐Shots start at the station you tied the survey to (✎ Edit on its card → Survey from here). The form suggests station names the cave does not have yet; a shot to a name that exists closes a loop onto that station and is drawn over the line already there. 🗺 Show on map takes the view to your shots.
🧭Declination is worked out offline (World Magnetic Model, for the survey's date) at the tie-in: “Declination 10.3° E (WMM 2026 at T6)”. Type your own (12 W) to override it; Use WMM goes back. Backsights shows the back readings; tap again to hide them.
📍Use my location takes one GPS fix (no network): the declination is worked out there for a survey tied to no station of the map, and its first station can be placed at the fix (approximate, marked ≈). ＋ New survey with no station selected starts at your location.
💾Display choices (walls, colour, wall opacity, stations, area names, terrain sliders) are remembered for this map. A new map opens with ghost walls coloured by elevation. The view stays over the map.
INVENTORIES
📦A chest at a station or a surface point keeps a dated log of what is stored there; the export carries them and Display → Inventories shows or hides them (a faded chest has something possibly out of date). Tap a chest, or the 📦 line on its station's card, for the contents table (item, quantity, last change, last checked) and the log (date, who, action, items, photos: tap one for full screen); a simple list shows as text. 🔍 finds an inventory by its name or by what is in it.
＋＋ Add entry is always there, like a note: left adds, took subtracts, checked sets the counts you saw (✓ Check… starts from the contents; leave “that was everything here” on and a line you take out counts as gone), note is text and photos only. The date is today, who is remembered, photos come from the camera or from files. Making an inventory (a station's card → ✎ Edit → 📦 Inventory), renaming it, keeping it as a simple list and changing or removing an entry wait behind the card's small ✎ Edit.
SHOTS FROM A SCANNED PAGE
📷🔧 → 📐 Field survey → 📷 Import shots from scan (or a document page's ⋯): photograph a page of shot tables, or pick a page or a file. FLEX Field reads it on the phone, offline; check the table beside the scan (tap a cell to see it on the page, tap the page to find its cell; orange = check, red = wrong; each column's menu says what it holds), choose ft or m, then Add … shots to a field survey. Each shot remembers its page. In a browser the page is shown beside an empty table to type into.
